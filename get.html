Install the iTeam beta (TestFlight)

TestFlight is Apple's free app for trying new versions. It's made by Apple and completely safe.

Step 1 · Get TestFlight Step 2 · Install iTeam beta
  1. Tap Step 1 → App Store opens → tap Get to install TestFlight.
  2. Come back here and tap Step 2 → TestFlight opens → tap Accept → Install.
  3. Open iTeam → make sure you're signed in to iCloud (Settings → your name) → type your name → Continue.
  4. To chat with Android friends: iTeam Settings → Privacy & security → Talk to Android users → Turn on with Apple ID.
  5. Add people: share your invite link or show your QR code (Chats → ＋).
Already have iTeam from the App Store? Installing the beta replaces it — your chats stay.

Install iTeam on Android

iTeam isn't on the Play Store yet, so you install it directly. Android shows a few safety warnings for any app from outside the Play Store — that's normal. Here's exactly what to tap.

Download iTeam for Android
  1. Tap the button — the download starts straight away (always the newest version).
  2. If Chrome says "file might be harmful" → tap Download anyway.
  3. Open the downloaded file (iTeam-Android.apk) from the notification or your Downloads folder.
  4. If you see "For your security, your phone is not allowed to install unknown apps from this source" → tap Settings → turn on Allow from this source → press back.
  5. Tap Install.
  6. If Google Play Protect shows "Unsafe app blocked" or "App not scanned" → tap More details → Install anyway. (Play Protect warns about every app that isn't on the Play Store yet.)
  7. Tap Open.
Samsung phones: if Auto Blocker stops the install, go to Settings → Security and privacy → Auto Blocker → turn it off for the install, then turn it back on.

Sign in & start chatting

  1. Open iTeam Next → type your name.
  2. Tap Continue with Google → pick your Google account. Google only confirms it's you — it can't read your messages.
  3. Pick your look (colour style) and see how to add people — a quick 2-step setup.
  4. Allow notifications when asked, so you see new messages.
  5. Add people: tap ＋ → Scan QR code, or paste the invite link a friend shared (it starts with iteam://add).
  6. iPhone friends: ask them to turn on Settings → Talk to Android users in iTeam first.
Updating? Download again from this page and tap Install → Update. Your chats stay. Turn on Settings → Backup & restore to keep a safe copy in Google Drive.
✅ Have the old "iTeam Chat" Android app? Keep or remove it — iTeam Next installs separately and is the one to use.
