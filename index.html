How to install

iPhone & iPad
    Android

      iTeam signs you in with an Apple Account — it keeps your chats private without needing your phone number. It's free and needs no Apple device.

      Delete your account

      In the app: Settings → Delete my account. This removes your public profile, Nearby listing and room memberships from iCloud and erases all iTeam data from your phone. Messages already delivered stay on the recipient's phone and are removed from iCloud within 45 days. Can't open the app? Email deven86@me.com from your Apple Account email and we'll delete your profile within 7 days.
